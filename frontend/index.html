Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do students need prior coding knowledge?

No. The first sessions start from the basics, and each track moves at a pace that suits beginners and students who already know a little.

How are the classes conducted?

Most sessions are live and virtual, on weekends or in the evening. Where possible, we also hold on-campus sessions at the institution.

What does Rs 200 per month include?

Live sessions with engineers, study notes, hands-on projects, hackathon participation, a monthly exam, and a certificate on completion.

Can my child join more than one track?

Yes. Students can join more than one track. Please contact us so we can plan the batch timings.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The introductory awareness workshop is free. Contact us to book a session at your school or college.
